# Engineering Notes

This document records concepts studied during the development of the RC airplane and how that knowledge is being applied to its electronic systems.

The videos below are learning references from the development process. They document individual learning activities and should not be interpreted as proof that a system has been fully integrated or tested in flight.

## MOSFET

**Learning focus:** Understanding how to use a MOSFET in an electronic circuit.

**Video:** [MOSFET learning video](https://youtu.be/2nw7uFwLbLg)

**Application to the project:** This learning is relevant to the airplane's propulsion electronics and motor control development. The specific circuit implementation and the MOSFET's role in the final design will be documented alongside the actual circuit and code.

## nRF24L01 Wireless Communication Module

**Learning focus:** Learning how to use the nRF24L01 module for wireless communication with Arduino.

**Video:** [nRF24L01 learning video](https://youtu.be/a25_9ffpNOA)

**Application to the project:** The Arduino and nRF24L01 are being developed as part of the airplane's wireless control electronics. The transmitter and receiver arrangement, data being exchanged, and code behavior will be explained in the nRF folder.
